# The Shattered Reach

The Shattered Reach is a broken coastline where the Ironspine Mountains
crash into the Verdant Sea. It is home to two rival powers: the mountain
clans of the highlands and the merchant cities of the shore.

## The Ironspine Mountains

The Ironspine Mountains form the spine of the Reach. Their peaks are
snow-locked for most of the year, and the passes close entirely in winter.
The mountain clans have lived here for a thousand years, mining silver and
raising hardy goats on the terraced slopes.

The largest peak, Mount Vashar, is considered sacred by the clans. No
outsider is permitted to climb it without the blessing of the Stone Mothers.

## The Coastal Cities

Three great cities line the coast: Saltmere, Bright Harbor, and Duskwatch.
Together they form the loose alliance known as the Tidewater League. Their
wealth comes from trade — silk from the south, spices from the eastern
islands, and silver bought (or seized) from the mountain clans.

Bright Harbor is the largest and wealthiest, ruled by an elected council of
merchant families. Duskwatch, by contrast, is a fortress-city, its harbor
guarded by the black towers that give it its name.
