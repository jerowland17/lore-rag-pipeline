# Alliances and Feuds of the Reach

The politics of the Shattered Reach are a tangle of old grudges and fragile
truces. Nothing here is permanent except the mountains and the sea.

## The Silver Accord

Fifty years ago, the mountain clans and the Tidewater League signed the
Silver Accord. In exchange for a fixed price on mountain silver, the coastal
cities agreed to leave the high passes unmolested. The Accord has held, but
only barely.

Clan Vashar, the largest of the highland clans, has grown resentful. They
believe the cities cheat them on weight and grade, paying for common ore the
price of dross.

## The Duskwatch Feud

Duskwatch and Clan Draymoor have feuded for three generations. It began with
a marriage that was promised and then broken, and it has since cost hundreds
of lives on both sides. Neither the Silver Accord nor the Tidewater League
has been able to end it.

Many fear that if open war returns to the Reach, it will begin at Duskwatch.

## The Neutral Parties

Bright Harbor styles itself the peacemaker, hosting envoys from both the
clans and the other cities. Its critics say this is merely a mask for
profit — Bright Harbor grows richest when the others are at each other's
throats.
