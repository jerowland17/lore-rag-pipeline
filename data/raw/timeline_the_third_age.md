# A Brief Timeline of the Third Age

The Third Age began with the fall of the Old Kingdom and is still unfolding.
What follows are the events most often recorded in the chronicles of the
Reach.

## Year 1 — The Sundering

The Old Kingdom collapsed when Mount Vashar erupted, splitting the coastline
and giving the Shattered Reach its name. Survivors scattered into the
highlands and along the broken shore, becoming the ancestors of the clans and
the city-folk.

## Year 210 — Founding of the Tidewater League

The three coastal cities, exhausted by decades of raiding, formed the
Tidewater League for mutual defense. Bright Harbor was named its first seat.

## Year 455 — The Silver Accord

After a bitter trade war, the mountain clans and the Tidewater League signed
the Silver Accord, fixing the price of silver and opening the high passes to
regulated trade.

## Year 501 — The Present

Today the Accord frays, the Duskwatch feud smolders, and the Stone Mothers of
Mount Vashar have begun to speak of omens. The chroniclers write that the
Reach stands on the edge of a knife.
